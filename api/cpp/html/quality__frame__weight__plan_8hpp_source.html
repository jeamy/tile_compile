3// QualityFrameWeightPlan --- milestone M3 (plan section 11.9). Records the
4// per-frame effective quality factor G_eff(f) and its three inputs, so that
5// G_eff(f) = G_quality(f)
6// * model_prediction_factor(f)
7// * registration_residual_factor(f)
8// is computed exactly once, before pixel reconstruction, and never a second
9// time inside the reconstructor (plan 11.9: "Eine doppelte Anwendung der
10// Registrierungsfaktoren in Pipeline und Rekonstruktor ist ausgeschlossen").
11//
12// The two registration factors are taken verbatim from the
13// RegistrationSamplingPlan (where runner_phase_registration already computed
14// and persisted them); GLOBAL_QUALITY only reads them here, never recomputes
15// them.
